# Set lists for the plates (look work, 2026-10-17)

Condensed from the district scripts (content/act1, content/act2), REWORK-PLAN, ACT1/ACT2 plans and
the bible, by two read-only passes. What each plate must show. Line refs are in the scripts.

## Act 1 (Night 1: fog 23:20, rain 23:35, heavy 01:30 to 02:40, blackout 02:50 to 03:20, frost 04:10, high water 05:10)
- **Quay**: three sodium swan-neck posts; five-storey crooked row (oxblood, mustard, teal, bone);
  bollards; Guild back wall with brackets; drainpipe over a herring barrel under a window; the
  steps "further than steps should" with the steps post (iron post, empty lantern cage, wick door);
  harbour office (window to the mole, cellar grating with two bolts and padlock on the quay);
  mole steps under a caged paraffin lamp; Brine back door and mangle; Congregation candles in jam
  jars and bell jars on the steps. Hides: under the steps, the gap of two lamps by the steps, fog,
  the dark end, the office doorway. Fights: e.lamplighters (wet stone under sodium, cut 3.5/10.5/17.5),
  e.wardens (bollards, the quay edge). High water 05:10 over the stones.
- **Harbour office and cells (one set)**: stove, chart under glass, chair with a hole over a bucket,
  telephone on the wall, harbour table on a nail, desk with paraffin lamp, window to the mole,
  cellar door. Cells six steps down: coils of wick, crate of mantles, wet bench, crate, grating to the quay.
- **Brine**: hull bar (stove, settle, Wenna's chair, brass rail, slate, pump, cracked brass clock,
  portholes, benches). Oven three steps behind the bar. Yard: barrels, mangle, bottle stack taller
  than a man, back door on a brick, oven ash door, bread peel, rain butt. Room above: round window,
  small stove, bed, bench (door on two barrels), plank wall for pins, ship's-ladder stair.
- **Weigh-house**: outside: switch box under the eave (handle, QUAY on the lid), office window (no
  catch) under the middle lamp, small latched window over the box, Board door with brass padlock,
  rain butt and drainpipe, chalk board and trestles. Inside: voting pans, chalk tallies, trestles,
  four lamps, weighbridge book on a chain, padlocked store (weights, chain, hooks, hammer, boathook),
  quay board (slate face, fuse). Office: green blotter, drain-hole chair over a basin, drawer.
  Tube: brass pipe to a brass mouth, wire basket, stool, lamp. Roof: lead, balance on a mast-thick post.
  Fights: e.afterdark (boards, bolted door), e.trial (a bench), e.officedoor.
- **Floor**: grey mud to the gully, black pools with upside-down lanterns, ribs of the Margery Dole,
  hand line rib to rib, stake at the gully lip, Lugg's rope across the gully, the drowned lamp (leans
  like a tooth, dressed stone, hinge-pins, hole with water, nail for a lantern, niche and locker,
  rubble skirt), the fish (scour, painted sign with a candle at its head), Nan Cockle's shingle bar.
  Fights: e.salvors (rubble round the tower), e.conger, e.gulls (mud by the fish), e.eels (mid-gully).
- **Mole and Mother**: stone arm, no rail windward, lee rail over a weed ledge, halfway bollard, the
  scab-coloured KEEPER door with boathook brackets, red pilot over the door; spiral stair round an
  iron post, landing at 56 with a slit window and a kitchen chair; lamp room (beehive lens, desk and
  log, brass panel with red button under glass, cupboard, rattling gallery door); gallery rail.
  Frost from 04:10. Fight e.hammer on frost by the lens.
- **Gut**: narrow lane, brine vats both walls, eel strings overhead, lookouts on fish boxes, back
  wall with brackets over Shadwell's gurry bin, Spratt's stall (plank on two barrels under an
  overhang), wire lamps behind coloured glass (red, amber, green, blue; every other one dark), the
  gutter (stone trough, slow violet bands), culvert (brick mouth under the quay), smokehouse door
  red, hall amber, stall green. Fight e.dogs in the midden yard (fish boxes, gurry bin).

## Act 2 (Night 2: clear, still, frost from 01:00, -2 C; low water 23:46; sea smoke 05:30)
- **Chancel Hill lane**: a white arc every twenty paces both sides, a church candle at each foot,
  no shadow except a foot-wide strip under the study sill; station halfway up (whitewashed, rack of
  poles, key board, shelf of black books, stove, desk, bench); Crake's house at the top, blinds down;
  substation (brick box, louvred window, padlock, hum, notice on the door, cable pole with outside
  fuse, duct grating). Arena: station 14 x 10, three white lamps on the top wall (x 3, 7, 11), door at the bottom.
- **Gasholder**: three rings of iron lattice on shingle, open to the stars; straw; candles in jam
  jars; gate and gate post; trestle altar with the Old Bulb on chains (violet glass, the size of a
  curled child) and the Eye (crown of prisms); stopcock at the west leg, pipe to a bracket by the
  Bulb; about forty kneelers in bell jars. Arena: shingle ring 20 x 11 round a raised dais, Bulb at
  centre, four candle lights, exits west and east.
- **Ropewalk**: long straight lane, one paraffin lamp halfway (arena has two at x 6 and 19 on 26 m),
  telegraph wire on the old rope posts, rope groove across the street, municipal-green telegraph
  door with a yellow window card, upstairs sash, Gannet house with a bright brass letter flap and a
  rocking step, yard-wide passage behind with a lit shutter and a warm grating (a dark stripe in the
  frost over the warm pipe). Frost from 01:00 keeps every print.
- **Spit**: two barges on their sides on shingle and weed (the Patience, the Ann Tull), bilge slope
  about 40 degrees with frost on the deck, open coal hatches, casks, a pool between them, the
  strippers' boat in a thigh-deep gut, Lamb's boat at the left edge; the Old Light leaning out of the
  water to the north, the hole toward the bay, nothing burning. Arena: 22 x 12, decks at the top
  centre and right, channel on the left, lantern at x 18 z 4.

## Daylight interlude (14:00 to 16:00, sun, tide out)
day_room (stripes of white light through the planking, coat by the stove, the plank wall),
the Brine bar (chairs on tables), day_quay (oxblood, mustard, teal, bone, five storeys leaning, red
washing across the lane, grey mud with pools holding the sky, Merrow's boat on the mud at the mole
steps, cells grating), day_mole (Mother a white tower with a green-and-gold head), day_tube (a barn
of a room, pans on the wall, balance chains through the roof), day_smoke (tar-black doors, eels,
gold smoked fish on rails).
