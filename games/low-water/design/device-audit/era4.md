# Era 4, The Closure: the device audit

Weighted-devices pass, 2026-10-23, branch `feat/wd-e4`. Part of [../DEVICE-AUDIT.md](../DEVICE-AUDIT.md),
which holds the systems, the era 2 decisions and the cross-era contract (section 3.4).

The era's hinge was a vote on paper and a map shaded in grey wash on an easel. Its denial was a report typed in a
police station. Both now happen on the street, to people, with the paint wet. The stakes are the same: the mill
shut, the Upper Town or the Row written off, Kasem hired at half pay a week before the closure or passed over, Lew
on the stair with the clinic's tablets, the Row burned or marked, Mira's family taken in or left on the prayer house
floor, Joss telling Kasem to go back, and the boxing club.

## 2. Plot changes

### 2.1 The map becomes a can of grey road paint and a walk

- **Was.** In the boardroom Victor Orme shaded one street on the bank's map in grey wash: the Row, or Exchange Street
  west. Ruth chose by arguing arrears on paper. Ann chose by letting Anzo carry his rent book into the boardroom.
- **Now.** The sale is a show of hands. Then Sell wants one more street after Haldane Yard, and Victor says he will
  not do it off a map: he walks it. He takes the surveyor's can of grey road paint off the easel, and they go down
  the hill to the Row, with the constable in front and the picket behind at a hearse's distance. On the Row the
  Electricity Board's man is coming out of number six with the fuse in his hand, and Teo stands in the dark doorway in
  his coat and hat, his breath showing. Victor says "This. Or the top end of Exchange Street."
  Ruth's choice is where she walks: she stays on the Row's kerb and looks at the ground (the Row is sprayed: "They
  can't keep the lights on, Arvid"), or walks back up the hill to her father's gate without looking round, and Victor,
  who said he would go where she went, sprays the kerb under Pell's pear tree while Pell watches from the step.
  Ann's choice is her hand: Anzo comes into the road with his broom in front of the can. She takes his arm and walks
  him back to his step, and Victor sprays the Row; or she goes and stands beside him, and Victor, with the whole
  street on its steps watching, turns round and does the top end instead. Anzo brings her a glass of tea.
- **Why.** The decision is made by a body, a hand and a can, in front of the people it falls on, and it leaves a
  mark on the street that the player meets again that night (the echo) and that Mira remembers at year 103. The grey
  of the old wash survives as the paint's colour, and Haldane Yard's stripe under the arch is the same mark era 5's
  clearance comes to.
- **Alternatives tested.** (a) Victor and Sell on the mill roof, choosing by where a hand points over the town:
  rejected, because the decision lands on a view and the people under it never see it, which repeats the boardroom's
  fault. (b) The surveyor marking doors with the can: rejected, because the Row's doors take the white crosses on the
  stood route and the goat is stencilled on the end wall; three marks on the same doors would blur who did what.
  The kerb stripe keeps the bank's mark apart from the mob's. (c) Ruth arguing aloud without figures ("the Row
  pays"): rejected, because the choice still turns on an account of money; walking keeps her flaw (she lets herself
  be followed, and calls it not choosing) in her feet.
- **Kept.** e4.map_row, e4.map_west, e4.board_sat, said.e4_ruth_west and said.e4_ruth_row (the same meaning: she
  steered it off her father's street, or onto it), said.e4_ann_anzo (she let Anzo make his stand). q.bank_map keeps
  its id and place (tests/props.test.mjs pins it at q4_board) and is now the can.

### 2.2 The hire is a hand on a shoulder and an envelope

- **Was.** Joss read the agency sheet off a clipboard; Ruth countersigned it and settled the hire by saying whether
  the agency line was in the budget.
- **Now.** Joss takes men with a tap on the shoulder. Ruth carries the run-down's wages in a cash box (a load) and
  pays them: thin envelopes for agency, fat ones for the old rate. She puts one in Joss's hand. Ann's lever is
  unchanged in kind: she vouches for Dale out loud, or tells him to get back in the line.
- **Why.** Half pay is felt in the hand, and the moment Joss's hand passes Kasem's shoulder is the decision.
- **Alternative rejected.** Joss decides alone with Ruth only watching: it removes the inspector's lever that the
  fate's "two ways" needs and her complicity with it.

### 2.3 The clinic is a key

- **Was.** Ruth shut the clinic early or kept it to the month's end by a line in the audit.
- **Now.** Hal puts the clinic cupboard's key on her desk. She pockets it, or slides it back to him.
- **Kept.** said.e4_ruth_early and said.e4_ruth_month, which decide Lew's night with the same logic.

### 2.4 The report becomes the burnt house, a witness and a word said aloud

- **Was.** Ann typed "electrical fault" from the brigade's sheet and rubbed Gedge's name out of her pocketbook.
  Ruth read the Gazette at her father's table.
- **Now.** The morning after is played on the Row for both classes. On the burned route the player walks the black
  hall (room.burnt), puts the light switch down out of habit and nothing happens, because the Board took the fuse the
  day of the walk. Mrs Bora from upstairs, who cannot hear, shows with her hands what she saw: two bottles, a ladder
  on a shoulder, the mill song whistled with no sound. Sergeant Rudd says "Electrical" to Paul Ashby on the pavement.
  Ann, asked, says "Electrical, Sarge", looking at the white on her glove. At the station desk the sergeant tells her
  to bin the bottle neck and she lets go of it over the bin. Ruth goes to the desk and tells what she saw; Rudd asks
  whether she saw him throw anything, says "Then you saw a man on a ladder", and drops the bag in the bin in front of
  her. On the stood route the same beats run on the white crosses and half a brick ("Persons unknown").
- **Why.** The denial is now an act by a person in front of the player, and its lie is something the player has
  felt with a finger on a dead switch. Ann's flaw stays fixed, as before: she conforms, now in her own voice.
- **Alternative rejected.** Giving Ann a choice to keep the bottle neck: a new branch with no downstream in eras 4
  and 5, and it would soften the flaw the arena returns to.

### 2.5 The pocketbook becomes the paint tin she held

Dale hands Ann his tin at the foot of the ladder and she holds it while he does the last letters. The white stays
in her glove. e4.lore.pocketbook keeps its id and is now that white on her thumb, found at the desk.

### 2.6 The poster becomes the stencilled goat and a chant

The goat sprayed under the defaced sign, with an arrow down the hill, points the night at the Row. The same goat is
wet on the Row's end wall when the bottle goes in (q.edrin_poster is that wall, with a wipe touch). "Edrin for
Edriners" is said by Dale at the Sheaf and taken up by the back tables. The police later hold the stencil
(q.goat_stencil), which keeps w_lore.lwd's chain w.goat.

### 2.7 The arrears card becomes the lights going off

The Board's man takes the fuse out of number six on the morning of the walk, in front of Teo, and the flat goes
cold (e4.lights.off, room.cold; flag e4.lights_out, echo e4.e.dark: a candle going from room to room that night).
The dead switch in the burnt hall makes "electrical" a lie the player has touched. q.meter_card keeps its id and is
now Teo's torch in Mira's tin at year 103, its switch rusted on. w_lore.lwd's chain w.meter ("No lights") reads the
same way: the torch, then the bag on the desk.

### 2.8 The papers on Anni's table become the seventh saucer

Mira lays the tea glasses from the birdcage on Anni's table and then the seventh saucer with no glass; Kasem says
"Aven". The fragment is now deep and open to both classes.

### 2.9 Mira's witness

Mira now sees what she reports: the lights, the can on the Row, the sergeant on the pavement. The paper beat is
`seen q4_row` and also tells e4.lights_out; the fire beat also tells e4.report_filed, and that unwitnessed line is
gone. PAUL is no longer a source; q4_police joins `never:`. The planted-boardroom check still applies (q4_board is in
`never:`).

### 2.10 Kept as it was

The station and the birdcage, the Sheaf (Joss's line on both Tomas routes), the pit pair, the last shift, the sign,
the fire itself, Anni's kitchen and chain H1, the boxing club and the narrow lens keep their beats; senses were
added (the wheel's brake, Lew's breathing, the room holding its breath, the window going in, the petrol catching,
Teo and Anzo carried as loads, the thread tied, the blow in the ring).
