# Act 2, Second Water, and the daylight interlude: the plan

From the director, 2026-10-17. Private. Built to the standard of `design/REWORK-PLAN.md`: five play
styles, the path matrix (its section 3.2), the verbs, fights that matter, the brevity rule. Act 2
was approved by the author on 2026-10-17. Writers read this after REWORK-PLAN.md.

## 1. The question and the shape

**Who pays?** The cause Lamb sent up the tube is a fact in Gullet, enforced by people who believe
the Shelf exists. Night 2 is clear and freezing: every lamp works and every face can be seen, so
the dark Lamb used on Night 1 has to be made.

| Movement | Clock | Weather | Turn |
|---|---|---|---|
| I. Clear Sky | 19:10 to 23:00 | Clear, still, 3 °C falling | The engineer arrives by the coast road (the ferry will not sail) and says why he wrote the clause |
| II. Second Water | 23:00 to 02:00 | Stars, -1 °C; low water 23:46 | The two ships stranded in Night 1's dark are being stripped, by the wreckers the claim invented |
| III. Hard Frost | 02:00 to 06:20 | Hard frost from 01:00, -2 °C; sea smoke 05:30 | The Shelf's machine with the sticking e is heard through a wall on the Ropewalk |

Ending: a second dawn. The Glass has forecast a gale. The town meets at the weigh-house to decide
which lamps it can light for it, and the lamps Lamb lit, mended, broke or left across two nights
decide what can be chosen.

## 2. The interlude, "Two in the Afternoon" (file `content/act2/day.lwd`, director)

Playable, short, in colour, no checks and no meters (REWORK-PLAN 5). Lamb wakes at 14:00 above the
Brine. Time for two of four calls before the light goes at 16:00, or sleep.

| Call | Node | Scene | Sets |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wenna on the mole | `day.wenna` | Mother's lens in sunlight: the green rings plain, nobody looking. She points; she says nothing about the night | Nothing. This call pays nothing (story editor S12): it is the one thing in the game that does not |
| Pip at the tube | `day.pip` | The afternoon canister, addressed to "the Gullet clerk". The sticking e | `day.pip`: the Ropewalk thread opens at 19:10 instead of 02:00 |
| Merrow | `day.merrow` | Mending a net on his boat, or behind the cells' grating, by the Act 1 cause | `day.merrow`: the Low Company's plan for the second low water |
| Marrow | `day.marrow` | Smoking the strike's fish in daylight for the first time in thirty years | `day.marrow`: the Guild's state of mind; Guild favour +1 or Spratt's plan |
| Sleep | `day.sleep` | The coat dries properly for the first time | Wear starts Night 2 at 0, and Lamb knows nothing new |

Small choices: one fact pinned to the wall (quoted by the first Night 2 scene); a second lantern
bought or not; a line sent to the Shelf by the afternoon tube or not (answered at 02:00). The image
at 16:00: the colours go out of the houses from the bottom of the hill up, like a tide coming in.

Same scene, same routes, same fight, same consequences. Lines that differ carry
`{when: act2_strippers == hesk}` or `== town`.

|---|---|---|
| Who | Strangers from Hesk, organised, armed with bars and hooks; the claim's invented wreckers made real by the story | Keel's tide-wardens and the Low Company's hard men under Sallis (or Lugg, if the Low Company was blamed), stripping "for the Shelf" |

Set: the strippers (spit), the Lamplighters' station or its talk (hill), the zealots round the Old
Bulb if a relic is taken (gasholder), the frozen gutter (gut2). Optional: Lamplighters in fours if
`book.violence`, the Trust's guards at the substation, a brawl over the Act 1 cause (brine2), the
Hesk men or wardens at the telegraph office. Gorm's pole sweep can be learned if the Trust reads
Lamb as useful. Every fight has its talk, sneak or tool route (REWORK-PLAN 7).

## 9. Protected moments in Act 2

   moving on them.

5. The sticking e through the wall.
