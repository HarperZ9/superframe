district into Night 2. Read `design/ACT2-WRITER-BRIEF.md` and follow it in full; this page adds
what each returning district holds. The night is clear and freezing (-2 °C from 01:00, hard
frost); every lamp works; the dark has to be made. Each file opens by reading the Act 1 state:
`cause.*`, `var.lens`, `var.fish`, `var.copper`, the lamp flags, `seenas_*`, favour, `struck.*`,
`book.violence`, `merrow.cells`, `appt.*`, the interlude's `day.*` flags and `day_pin`.
