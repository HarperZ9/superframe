# Literary bible 5a: the author's game list, method by method

Part of [../LITERARY.md](../LITERARY.md), beside [05-INSPIRATION.md](05-INSPIRATION.md). The author,
2026-10-22, verbatim: "Disco Elysium, Zero Parades: For Dead Spies, No Rest For the Wicked, No, I'm Not a
Human, Eclipsium, Labyrinth of the Demon King, Hail to the Rainbow, Rue Valley, Mouthwashing, Threshold,
Carimara: Beneath the Forlorn Limbs, Shadowgate, Elden Ring, Neva, Gris, Omori, Fear and Hunger, FEar and
Hunger 2: Termina, SKALD: Against the Black Priory, Kenshi, Felvidek, Banquet for Fools, Judero,
Planescape: Torment, Blasphemous 1 & 2, etc."

**Rules.** Method only, never content: no names, plots, characters, lines, art, music or mechanics copied
as designs. **Facts.** Titles marked *verified* were checked by web search on 2026-10-22 (sources at the
end); *memory* means from the story lead's knowledge, with a confidence label; *unverified* means the
basic fact could not be confirmed and nothing is built on it. No plot is guessed: where this file names
a method, it names a structural property the sources or long-standing public knowledge describe.

## The list

**Disco Elysium** (ZA/UM, 2019; memory, high). *Borrow:* skills as speaking characters that argue with the
protagonist; politics named plainly in an invented world; failure that opens a scene. *Maps onto:* the
sixteen voices (`q_rules:48-127`), the self-lie tags, the slice's checks. *Avoid:* the quotable epigram
(DIALOGUE-STANDARD section 4, after the author rejected exactly that register); copying its skill names.

**Zero Parades: For Dead Spies** (ZA/UM; verified: released on PC on 21 May 2026). *Borrow:* nothing
specific; the story lead has not played it. The design's earlier aim stands: a world read through its
things (PITCHES 3c). *Maps onto:* the world media layer (MEDIA). *Avoid:* claims about its systems until
someone has played it (unverified).

**No Rest for the Wicked** (Moon Studios; memory, high). *Borrow:* combat feel only, by the author's
ruling: committed attacks, readable telegraphs, weight. *Maps onto:* the granary door and the arena.
*Avoid:* its world and story.

**No, I'm Not a Human** (Trioskaz, published by Critical Reflex; verified: Steam, 15 September 2025).
*Borrow:* the player decides who to let in, guided by daily broadcasts that list traits for spotting an
impostor. This is the method of manufactured suspicion: the authority teaches you the signs, and you start
looking for them on your neighbours. *Maps onto:* the hoarding notice and its carriers (`q_discourse:21-29`);
Holt reading Tomas's wrist (`q_ned:174`); era 5's clip comments. PROPOSAL: an era 2 circular, "How to know a
hoarder", whose signs (fat on the clothes, a braid, a thread) the player can apply at the post. *Avoid:* any
version where the signs turn out to be true of a people. Our signs describe a people and catch the innocent.

**Eclipsium** (Housefire, published by Critical Reflex; verified: Steam, 19 September 2025; first person,
surreal, puzzles that bend perception, lo-fi PS1-style look). *Borrow:* a surreal shift that changes the
rules of perception for a stretch, then returns. *Maps onto:* the dream (`q_dreams:29-37`), where the table
may run past the wall ([03-REGISTER.md](03-REGISTER.md)). *Avoid:* body horror applied to any people
(MEDIA rule 4 forbids disease and animal bodies on images of a people).

**Labyrinth of the Demon King** (J.R. Hudepohl, published by Top Hat Studios; verified: 13 May 2025,
first-person dungeon crawler with survival horror). *Borrow:* dread from scarcity: every resource spent is
felt. *Maps onto:* the famine day's economy; the bread in Will's pocket; the arena's loot with a source on
every reward (pillar 6). *Avoid:* jump scares (the author's taste: "tense, not jump scares", ART-DIRECTION
section 2).

**Hail to the Rainbow** (Sergey Noskov; verified: Steam, 27 November 2025; a first-person story game in an
Eastern European apocalypse, by a solo developer). *Borrow:* only what is verified: a place-first, first-person
atmosphere built by one maker. *Maps onto:* the wide lens at dawn. *Avoid:* everything else until played;
its plot is unverified here.

**Rue Valley** (Emotion Spark Studio; verified: an isometric narrative RPG about a man in a time loop of
forty-seven minutes, whose choices shape his personality; press describes it as about depression).
*Borrow:* the repeated short span where what changes is the person, never the day. *Maps onto:* the world
record across runs: day 41 played again, with the previous character's line chalked at the post
(`q_props:122`). *Avoid:* the loop as a puzzle box. The Quota's repetition is moral, and the day never
resets for the Haldanes in the record.

**Mouthwashing** (Wrong Organ, published by Critical Reflex, 2024; memory, high on the form). *Borrow:*
a non-linear telling that makes the player realise late what the protagonist did, and the complicity of
having played him. *Maps onto:* the read-back's two columns (`q_readback:37-62`); the self-lie the player
believed first. *Avoid:* its specific revelations; shock as the payload. Our handling rules keep sexual
violence to aftermath only (07-PRODUCTION, content warning).

**Threshold** (Julien Eveille, published by Critical Reflex; verified: Steam, 19 November 2024; a border
guard on a mountain post keeps an endless train moving; every step and whistle costs oxygen; a colleague
whose answers are not always reliable). *Borrow:* a functionary at a line, where doing the job is itself
the drain. *Maps onto:* Will at the road post (`q_will:67-169`); Gedge as the colleague who watches back
("That goes in the log, Sergeant. Or it goes to the Captain. You pick.", `q_will:157`). *Avoid:* the state
as pure abstraction. Pask and Holt have names, colds and quotas.

**CARIMARA: Beneath the Forlorn Limbs** (Bastinus Rex, published by Critical Reflex; verified: Steam, 6
October 2025; a mute creature speaks through a deck of cards; Norman folklore; stop-motion; the story
emerges fully only at the end). *Borrow:* speech through a small fixed set of objects. *Maps onto:* Anni,
whose tallies speak for her (`q_ned:89-90`); PROPOSAL: a Hesh child in era 3, forbidden Heshen at school,
who answers with tally marks. *Avoid:* withholding the whole story to the last minute in a 15-hour game.

**Shadowgate** (ICOM Simulations, 1987; memory, high). *Borrow:* the verb-and-object interface (examine,
open, take) as the main way of reading the world; light that runs out as a clock. *Maps onto:* the examine
verb and props (`q_props`); the day's clock (`slice.lwd:13-14`). *Avoid:* sudden deaths and game-over
cards (the design excludes them, QUOTA-DESIGN "the frame").

**Elden Ring** (FromSoftware, 2022; memory, high). *Borrow:* history held in item and place descriptions,
fragmentary and consistent; bosses with learnable patterns. *Maps onto:* the lore graph (`q_lore`); the
arena's bosses. *Avoid:* lore so oblique it needs a wiki. `lorecheck` already requires every chain to be
completable.

**Neva** (Nomada Studio, 2024; memory, high). *Borrow:* a wordless story across seasons, where a place
loses its colour. *Maps onto:* the era transitions, told without words through the tower view.
*Avoid:* using an animal companion as the emotional shortcut.

**Gris** (Nomada Studio, 2018; memory, high). *Borrow:* emotional state carried by colour and space, with no
dialogue. *Maps onto:* the per-era looks (see `design/SENSORY.md`); the blue thread as the one saturated
colour ([03-REGISTER.md](03-REGISTER.md)). *Avoid:* grief resolved by a palette arc. The Quota does not
heal.

**OMORI** (OMOCAT, 2020; memory, high). *Borrow:* a soft surface over a buried act the player uncovers;
dream and waking as two lenses on one guilt. *Maps onto:* "He went to the hills" (`HOUSEHOLDS:85`) as a
family's soft surface; the dream. *Avoid:* the twist as the point.

**Fear & Hunger** (Miro Haverinen, 2018) **and Fear & Hunger 2: Termina** (2022) (memory, high on the
form). *Borrow:* an indifferent, harsh world where losses are permanent; in Termina, a countdown over days
with a whole cast whose fates run in parallel. *Maps onto:* pillar 1 (no reloading around reckoning
choices); the community model's households, whose end states run whether the player looks or not.
*Avoid:* their gore and sexual cruelty for edge. Our rule: "No cruelty for edge" (THEMES.md section 11).

**SKALD: Against the Black Priory** (High North Studios, 2024; memory, moderate). *Borrow:* a narrated
CRPG where prose boxes carry the scene beside small art. *Maps onto:* the narration between portrait
dialogue. *Avoid:* retro pastiche.

**Kenshi** (Lo-Fi Games, 2018; memory, high). *Borrow:* a world that runs without the player, where the
player is nobody special. *Maps onto:* the community model's daily tick; Decay "no hero framing"
(PITCHES, Decay constraint 5). *Avoid:* empty grinding.

**Felvidek** (Jozef Pavelka; verified: 29 March 2024; a knight with a drinking problem in a low-fantasy
fifteenth-century Slovakia). *Borrow:* a drinker protagonist written with humour and regional specificity,
and history made local. *Maps onto:* Will's bottle "off a cart" (`q_dinner:123-125`); the plain's own
dialect (QUOTA-VOICES 11.2). *Avoid:* drink as only comic; the Brack table shows what it does to Davey
(`q_dinner:190`).

**Banquet for Fools** (Hannah and Joseph Games; verified: 1.0 on Steam, 5 March 2026; claymation-style
party RPG; an island recently settled by colonisers; a farm's residents vanish; investigation without hand
holding). *Borrow:* investigation the player must do, without markers. *Maps onto:* the missing-Tomas chain
held across classes (`q_lore:6-13`). *Avoid:* its premise. A vanished farm household is close to ours;
keep Tomas's chain on its own terms and never borrow its structure of discovery.

**Judero** (Talha Kaya and Jack King-Spooner; verified: 16 September 2024; stop-motion, handmade models,
folk history of the Scottish Borders). *Borrow:* handmade material texture, and folk history kept local.
*Maps onto:* Hesh craft in the world media (the spoons and the weaving, `MEDIA:71-72`). *Avoid:* folk
history as decoration.

**Planescape: Torment** (Black Isle, 1999; memory, high). *Borrow:* a protagonist answerable for what his
earlier selves did, found in their writing; dialogue as the main verb. *Maps onto:* the family trade: each
generation inherits the last one's record (PITCHES section 1), and across runs the player's earlier
character is the earlier self. *Avoid:* its length of line; our brevity rule wins.

**Blasphemous** and **Blasphemous II** (The Game Kitchen, 2019 and 2023; memory, high). *Borrow:* guilt and
penance as the world's religion, rendered grotesque; lore in relic descriptions. *Maps onto:* the Furrow
Church's iconography, "the Sower" covered through the famine (`MEDIA:63`). *Avoid:* suffering as spectacle;
mapping a real faith one to one (THEMES.md section 11).
