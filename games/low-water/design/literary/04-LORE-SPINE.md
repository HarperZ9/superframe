## 3. The open questions, held deliberately

Writers must never answer these in any line, prop or read-back. Each is held by two pieces of evidence
that disagree.

1. **Who did the guards take when the door held?** Bet tells herself the Orme lad, by the coat
   (`q_perspective:61`); the read-back says only "The guards took a man to show for the night"
   (`q_readback:23`); the granary scene shows Tomas (`q_granary:91`). The game never confirms
   (`q_perspective:5-6`).
2. **Was the dream a premonition or the count?** "THE DARK: it is what is coming" against "SUM: it is the
   day's count" (`q_dreams:18-19`). It comes true on most runs and fails on some.
3. **Where is Tomas?** "He went to the hills" (`HOUSEHOLDS:85`); the corner (`q_lore:73`); in era 5, under
   the verge (`q_props:64`). Even a player who holds every fragment never sees the body.
4. **Did Abel love Ilo?** "I'd have trusted him with my life. Not with my grain." (`q_dinner:81`). Both
   halves are meant.
5. **Why is 41 everywhere?** The day, Ned's age, the crowd (see [02-DEVICES-B.md](02-DEVICES-B.md)
   section 16). PROPOSAL: keep it as the one unexplained number.
6. **What did the Wether pass cost?** "The Captain's signed it. The pass." (`q_coda:193`). Will said he
   would (`q_coda:194`). Nothing says what Will gave for it. PROPOSAL: never say.
7. **Who carved the goat on the table leg?** Assumed Ilo; nothing says so (`q_props:12`).
8. **Was the honest figure worth it?** The coda gives Tarn Hollow Sallow's 320 (`q_coda:138`) and the
   Furrowe bowls a quarter short (`q_coda:135`). The game keeps the two books separate (PITCHES, Decay,
   "the two keep separate books") and never totals them.
9. **Who is Anni's father? ANSWERED 2026-10-22.** The author: "You decide, and expand upon it deeply."
   The coordinator decided: Ned Furrowe, at about 27 and promised to Mary, with Bet about 20 and working
   at the Furrowe farm; Abel covered it with the match to Ilo and the Haldane barns tenancy; Edwin Mott
   ruled the father column; Ilo knew and took the bargain (`HOUSEHOLDS:77-87`). It leaves this list as a
   question and stays on it as a rule for writers: no line, caption, prop or read-back row says it, and
   no character says "father" with Ned's name. The player reads it from objects and faces. What stays
   open in its place: **could Bet refuse him?** Her account and Ned's never agree, and the narration
   never rules (`design/spine-plan/09-ANNI.md` sections 2, 3 and 5).

## 4. Lore to build for the long game (PROPOSALS)

- **The Furrowe notebook, era 2.** The design's line, "Went up with the boy to see the hole. There was no
  hole. Reported as found." (PITCHES.md line 376), is not in the slice, where the notebook says "Tesk's
  fold, day 41. No store." (`q_ned:246`). Keep the slice's version for day 41 and put the PITCHES line in
  a later entry, dated after the review, so the notebook itself shows the change from honest pencil to
  ink lie.
- **The census register, era 1.** The page where "Ilo" becomes "Haldane", and the ration card twelve
  years later that misspells it "HALDENE", with "the a over the e in pencil, pressing so hard it has gone
  through the card" (`q_props:73`). The name was given wrong and corrected by hand by the family.
- **The children's home book, Tilney Road.** The Furrow Church's intake book, era 2: Anni's name, her
  tin of tallies listed as property, and a column for "released to family" that is filled for some
  children and not others.
- **The commemoration, era 5.** The memorial says "the hard winter" (`MEDIA:79`). The archive's minutes
  of the committee that chose those words, with the Furrowe line's member present.

## 5. Continuity faults found (for the owners; not fixed here unless marked RESOLVED)

1. **Sefa Tesk's age.** Davey says Sefa Tesk is at school in era 2 (`q_dinner:186`). The design has Sefa
   Tesk as an elder of the Well Assembly in era 5 (`HOUSEHOLDS:96-97`), 103 years later. Either the era 2
   boy is a namesake (a grandfather's name passed down, which the Hesh could plausibly do) or one of the
   two is renamed. Recommendation: make it a family name passed down and let era 5's Sefa say so.
2. **The Haldane years. RESOLVED 2026-10-22** by the author: "Make Tomas about 11 and Aaron about 9 in
   era 2 and rewrite the affected lines and records." Tomas and Aaron are born after era 1's wedding
   (Tomas 11, Aaron 9; `q_world:212`, `q_world:224`), Tomas's line to Ned ("before I was born",
   `q_ned:85`) is true, and the scenes and records were rewritten for a boy (PROGRESS step 171). Anni is
   fourteen in era 2 and era 2 stays twelve years after era 1, so she is born about two years before the
   wedding. The author's second ruling, the same day, answers that: "Out of wedlock." Bet had Anni before
   she married Ilo; the register's father column is blank; Ilo raised her as his own and she carries the
   Haldane name (`HOUSEHOLDS:76-79`, PROGRESS step 173). Her father is Ned Furrowe (section 3, question
   9; `design/spine-plan/09-ANNI.md`). The original finding follows. Tomas was nineteen in era 2
   (`q_world:212`), so seven in era 1. But era 1 is when Ilo "works two summers for Abel Furrowe", is
   given his surname and "marries Bet" (`HOUSEHOLDS:74-76`), and Tomas says his father worked for Abel
   "before I was born" (`q_ned:85`). The table and the bowls are "bought together at Osby fair with the
   wedding money" in era 1 (`q_props:25`). Either era 1 moves about twenty years before era 2, or Tomas is
   about eleven, or Ilo's summers and the wedding come before era 1 and era 1 shows the census naming of
   a man already married. Recommendation (superseded by the author's ruling): era 1 at year minus 20,
   which also lets Abel be in his prime at the exchange.
3. **Pell's age.** Eleven in the voice table (QUOTA-VOICES line 49); twelve in the scene ("I'm twelve",
   `q_dinner:111`) and in the record (`q_world:146`).
4. **The cell log's last column.** PITCHES 3c has "released to the inspector" (PITCHES.md line 375);
   the slice prop has "The column for released is ruled and empty" (`q_props:113`). The slice is
   the canon; the PITCHES table is older.
5. **NELL and DAVY.** QUOTA-VOICES 11.1 and 11.3 still use the proposed names; the content uses Edie and
   Davey (`q_world:178-188`), and 11.7 records the change. The 11.3 table should follow.
