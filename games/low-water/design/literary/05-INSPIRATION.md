# Literary bible 5: inspiration and media map

Part of [../LITERARY.md](../LITERARY.md). Every entry names a real work and says what is borrowed in
method. Nothing is borrowed in content: no names, plots, characters, lines, images, melodies or designs.
All Low Water work is original and clean-room (THEMES.md section 13; the clean-room rule in the
production bar). Facts about the works are from memory unless marked; confidence is given per row where
it matters. The touchstones the author named come first.

## 5. History (research anchors, never named in the game)

The anchors in PITCHES.md section 5 stand: Bengal 1943, Ukraine and Kazakhstan 1932 to 1933, China 1959
to 1961, Sen's free-press claim, redlining and urban renewal, enclosure, pass laws, Jim Crow, colonial
extraction, deindustrialisation, the original ghetto. Two methods from them are underused:
- **The purge of the honest official** (China, 1959; high confidence on the mechanism, already in the
  anchor table). After one man spoke the true figure and was removed, nobody else did. The slice has
  this in Ned's coda (`q_coda:135`). Era 3 should show it as a rule people now follow without being told.
- **The list** (every anchor). Administrative lists made the harm possible. Pask's "a list of the Hesh
  families on your side of the river" (`q_office:72`) is the slice's most dangerous line, and its
  read-back row (`q_readback:83`) should follow the list into era 3.
