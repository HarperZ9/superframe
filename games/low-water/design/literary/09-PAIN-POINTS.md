humanity, that the work treats or has been asked to treat. Sources: THEMES.md (six questions, five axes,
the personal axis), the Quota design and slice, and the author's work of the last months

flinches, and a proposal that hits hard without a lecture. Sample lines are PROPOSALS. Handling rules
(PITCHES "Perspectives" and 3b) govern every row: aftermath for sexual violence, consequence for harm to

## How to hit hard without lecturing

1. **The person pays first, the idea never.** Every pain point lands on a named household in the model
   (pillar 3, "Victims have faces"). If a row above cannot name who pays, it is not ready to write.
2. **One witness per harm.** Anni counting is the template (`q_ned:150`): somebody says it so somebody
   has said it, and is not thanked.
3. **The comfortable sentence first.** Each pain point has a reasonable line that defends it, written to
   be believed (PITCHES, "What they tell themselves", rule 2), before the cost arrives.
4. **The author's side pays too.** Rows 5, 7 and 23 aim at the author's own tools and principles on
   purpose (see [06-ALLEGORY-AUDIT.md](06-ALLEGORY-AUDIT.md) section 3).
5. **No row ends on a moral.** The scene ends on a smaller or worse detail (DIALOGUE-STANDARD 3a).
