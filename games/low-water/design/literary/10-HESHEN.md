# Heshen: the words in play (era 2)

Game director, 2026-10-22. The first Heshen to reach the player, in the Hesh-only walk home and the Sheaf
(`content/quota/q_sheaf.lwd`) and in the prayer the boy says wrong in the cells (`content/quota/q_cells.lwd`).
Invented, not borrowed: no word is taken from a living language. Never glossed on screen; a player learns a
word from who says it and when. This list is for writers, so a word means the same thing in every era.

## Sound

Soft stops, open vowels, an "sh" and an "l" more often than Tarnish has them. Stress on the first syllable.
Statements rise at the end in speech, which is how the Tarn hear a Hesh accent in Tarnish (voice sheets).

## The words in era 2

| Word | Sense | Where it is used |
|---|---|---|
| sul | water | The prayer for the water; Ilo's "Sul ve ama" leaving the Sheaf |
| ve | before, in front of | The prayer |
| ama | me (object), the speaker | The prayer; "Sul ve ama" said leaving a place: water before me |
| tarre | behind | The prayer |
| ven | at, by | The prayer's end |
| ishe | the last, the end of a thing | The prayer's end |
| kesh | enough, stop that, hush | Tomas on the road; Bet to the children |
| ahna | mother, as a child says it | Tomas on the road |
| Sulwe | the Well, the faith (proper noun) | Not yet spoken in era 2; the Tarn say "the Well" |

## The prayer for the water

Said at night, and over the dead before they go into the ground with water. Four lines. The game prints only
the first two, in Bet's mouth on the walk home. The end is "sul ven ishe dar", water at the last of it. The
boy in the end cell says "sul ven ishe" and stops, which is the end wrong: nobody taught him the last word,
because his father learned the prayer late and his mother is Tarn. Dorrah says the end across the corridor.

## Rules for writers

- No gloss in narration, captions or the interface. A character may translate for a Tarn listener, and the
  translation may be wrong.
- One new word per scene at most, and only where a Hesh speaker would reach for it: kin, water, the Well,
  hushing a child, rain.
- The nine words for rain are named by the Tarn joke and by Tomas's answer to it. They are not listed in play.
- Era 3 bans Heshen in school. From then on the words appear in kitchens and at gravesides, and less each era.
