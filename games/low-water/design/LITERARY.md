# Low Water: the literary bible (index)

Story lead, 2026-10-22. A working bible for the writers and for the systems: the game's themes,
motifs, lore, inspirations, metaphors and symbols, each device applied to The Quota as written. Read after
QUOTA-DESIGN.md, design/quota/, THEMES.md and the slice in content/quota/.

**Rules that bind this bible.** It edits no existing content or scene. It quotes the content with
`file:line` and invents no facts about it; anything new is marked **PROPOSAL** and waits for the author.
Dialogue proposals meet DIALOGUE-STANDARD.md (the read-aloud test, concrete stakes, no epigrams). Looks,
transitions, audio and haptics belong to `design/SENSORY.md` (the art and sensory agent); this bible
covers only what the writing calls for there.

## The strongest findings

1. **Diction carries the family lies, and each lie is born live in era 2.** Mott's "Everyone's writing
   three hundred" (`content/quota/q_ned.lwd:39`), Ned's "I add up what they send in" (`q_dinner.lwd:42`)
   and Will's "Somebody had to keep order" (`q_lore.lwd:77`) are the era 5 family lies of
   design/quota/02-HOUSEHOLDS.md, first said as reasonable sentences on day 41.
2. **"Only" is the Furrowe inheritance.** Abel's "I'm only saying" (`q_dinner.lwd:81`) and Ned's run of
   "it's only" (`q_ned.lwd:156`, `q_coda.lwd:141`), turned inside out by Anni's "I'm only saying it so
   somebody's said it" (`q_ned.lwd:150`).
3. **Pencil and ink are the slice's best metaphor**: the truth kept where it can be denied
   (`q_ned.lwd:52`, `q_ned.lwd:63`, `q_ned.lwd:248`), and it can carry all five eras.
4. **The hand on the hidden thing binds perpetrator and victim.** Will "keeps putting his hand on it to
   see if it's still there" (`q_will.lwd:12`); Ilo, with the death paper, the same words (`q_coda.lwd:63`).
5. **The best dramatic irony is Bet's coat**: she tells herself the man taken was the Orme lad by his
   coat (`q_perspective.lwd:61`), and the player saw Tomas put it on (`q_perspective.lwd:33`).
6. **Situational irony that does not reward honesty**: the true figure gets Anni hired to count her own
   family's sacks into the brigade's store (`q_coda.lwd:92`).
7. **Taste is missing from a game about hunger.** Food is counted and seen and never tasted.
8. **The victims never speak among themselves, and have no contempt of their own**, which leaves PITCHES
   3a rule 4 unmet and the Hesh as spectators' victims.
9. **The number 41 repeats unexplained** (the day, Ned's age at `q_world.lwd:134`, the crowd at
   `q_perspective.lwd:34`), and it echoes the archived Gullet dream's forty-one ticks.
10. **Two continuity faults**: Sefa Tesk is a schoolboy in era 2 (`q_dinner.lwd:186`) and an elder 103
    years later; the Haldane ages do not fit a twelve-year gap between eras 1 and 2.
11. **The author's mission is on trial in the slice already**: checking was cheap (Ned counted the barns
    himself), and saying it was what cost. The game's own read-back is also a record with no release.

## The top ten proposals

1. **A Hesh-only scene per era**, starting with the Haldanes walking home from the post (08-CRAFT
   section 2), and Dorrah's line drawn at the goat-Hesh children (04-LORE-SPINE section 2).
2. **Dramatic irony through the world record**: lines whose meaning exists only for a player holding a
   prior fact, two or three per era (02-DEVICES-B section 11).
3. **Dreams that run an era ahead**, showing the next era's prop state, true only if the model makes it so
   (02-DEVICES-B section 8).
4. **Pencil and ink across five eras** as the motif of what can be rubbed out, with a "rub" option that
   enters the record (03-REGISTER).
5. **One scene of ordinary pleasure before every hinge**, starting with Tomas, sent to fetch Ilo home, making
   the back table at the Sheaf laugh on day 40 (02-DEVICES-B section 18).
6. **Frank Mott asks Mara to tear out his father's page** before the archive opens, the author's
   records-and-release thesis put to the player with no side taken (06-ALLEGORY-AUDIT section 3.5).
7. **Self-lies for the other four classes and a victim's column in the read-back** (08-CRAFT section 3).
8. **A Heshen word list, scripture fragments for both faiths, Directorate poster words and a plain
   calendar** (08-CRAFT section 4).
9. **"How to know a hoarder"**, a circular whose signs the player can apply at the post, after the
   broadcast method of No, I'm Not a Human (05a-GAMES).
10. **Holt as the recurring antagonist with one scene where he is right** ("I'll have to take it back off
    Osby", 01-DEVICES-A section 4), and Halvard given an ordinary face on the train (07-VOICE-GUIDE).
