# The Quota, 2: the households

Part of [QUOTA-DESIGN.md](../QUOTA-DESIGN.md). Ten households followed across five eras, like a family
panel in a survey. The six family-trade lines are the player's classes; the player plays one generation
of the chosen line in each era. The Haldanes are the narrow lens's family.

Generations run about 25 years; eras 1 to 3 fall inside two generations, eras 4 and 5 jump. Each line
below names the person the game follows in that era.

**End states,** the same five for every household, each with its own short scene (about fifty in all,
authored, PITCHES.md 3c): **rose** (stayed and did well), **fell** (stayed and went under), **left**,
**ended** (the line has no one left in Edrin), **absorbed** (married into, renamed, passing as another
people). The community model decides which, from the household's stocks and the record.

## The six family-trade lines (the classes)

### Mott, the Clerks
- **Era 1** Edwin Mott, census clerk, who writes the rule that seasonal hands are not residents.
- **Era 2** Walter Mott, brigade clerk for Krall's End and the Haldane barns. Overwrites 190 to 310. Writes
  his sevens without a bar, as his father did.
- **Era 3** Walter, older, the allocation office's clerk.
- **Era 4** Irene Mott, the council's records clerk, who files the bank's map.
- **Era 5** Old Frank Mott, retired, at the Haldane kitchen table with the ledger (PITCHES rev 4 sample).
- **Wound:** the ledger. **Lie:** "Everyone was writing three hundred." **Exclusive:** the original
  ledger; the census board's minutes.

### Furrowe, the Inspectors
- **Era 1** Abel Furrowe, exchange inspector, who has a good hat and is proud of it. Covers his son's
  child by Bet: the match with Ilo, the Haldane barns tenancy, the census entry.
- **Era 2** Ned Furrowe, district inspector; wife Mary; son Pell; Abel at the table (the 4.1 dinner). Goes
  up the hill with Tomas Haldane. Writes "Reported as found." Anni Haldane's father, which he has never
  said; he counts the Haldane barns himself every September (`design/spine-plan/09-ANNI.md`).
- **Era 3** Pell Furrowe, reconstruction surveyor. Opens the door to Anni, his half-sister, and does not
  know her.
- **Era 4** Ruth Furrowe, Halvard's auditor for the mill closure.
- **Era 5** Nan (Ruth's daughter), her children Jo and Tom ("Dad"), Tom's daughter Kit (the 4.2 clip).
- **Wound:** the hill. **Lie:** "We only reported what the villages sent." **Exclusive:** the notebooks;
  the maps with the real figures pencilled.

### Ashby, the Correspondents
- **Era 1** Clement Ashby, the exchange's press gallery. **Era 2** Lena Ashby, who photographs the one full
  granary twice for the capital's paper and has the negative of the boy at the granary door spiked.
  **Era 3** Lena, writing the schoolbook's chapter "The Plain Rebuilt". **Era 4** Paul Ashby, the campaign
  office's writer. **Era 5** Ellie Ashby, an influencer with a channel about "the real Edrin".
- **Wound:** the photograph. **Lie:** "I showed what they let me show." **Exclusive:** the spiked story;
  the negatives.

### Lind, the Doctors
- **Era 1** Dr Oskar Lind, the exchange's doctor. **Era 2** Dr Clara Lind, who writes "intestinal illness"
  four hundred times and dates Tomas Haldane's certificate two days early. **Era 3** Clara, at the fever
  ward. **Era 4** Dr Hal Lind, the mill's occupational clinic. **Era 5** Dr Sam Lind, who runs the Upper
  Town vaccine centre.
- **Wound:** the certificates. **Lie:** "I wrote what would let the families bury them." **Exclusive:** the
  death certificates; the quarantine lists.

### Brack, the Militia and the police
- **Era 1** Josh Brack, the road post. **Era 2** Sergeant Will Brack, who holds Tomas in the cells and,
  drunk and grateful, tells a player who blamed the Hesh where "the hoarder" was put. Comes home and turns
  on his wife and children (the Perspectives pair "the house"). **Era 3** Will, at the allocation riot.
  **Era 4** Constable Ann Brack, on the picket line. **Era 5** Sergeant Leon Brack, the stop-and-search van
  on Mill Street; his knee is in the clip.
- **Wound:** the cell. **Lie:** "Somebody had to keep order." **Exclusive:** the cell logs; the patrol
  orders.

### Orme, the Millers and bankers
- **Era 1** George Orme, the mill and the exchange's biggest buyer. **Era 2** George, holding sacks back and
  selling at night. **Era 3** Harriet Orme, the rebuilding loans. **Era 4** Harriet's son Victor, who sells
  the mill to Halvard and joins the bank's board that draws the map. **Era 5** Charlotte Orme, of the
  ring-road syndicate.
- **Wound:** the night carts. **Lie:** "The price is the price." **Exclusive:** the grain held back; the
  bank's map.

## The other four households

### Haldane (the narrow lens)
- **Era 1** Ilo, a Hesh hand in the barns who works two summers for Abel Furrowe ("lovely lad, sang all
  day", the 4.1 dinner). The census clerk Edwin Mott enters him as Haldane, after the farm, because the
  register needs a Tarn name. He marries Bet, a Tarn cottar's daughter. Bet already has a daughter, Anni,
  about two years old, born out of wedlock (author, 2026-10-22: "Out of wedlock."). Her father is Ned
  Furrowe (the author: "You decide, and expand upon it deeply."; decided by the coordinator, 2026-10-22):
  Ned was about 27 and promised to Mary, Bet about 20 and working at the Furrowe farm. Whether she could
  refuse him stays contested, and no line rules on it. Abel covered it: he married Bet to Ilo and gave
  Ilo the Haldane barns tenancy, which also kept Ilo off the hill when the census rule made hands
  non-residents. Edwin Mott ruled the father column through. Ilo knew, took the bargain, and loves her.
  Anni is Tarn by blood, raised Hesh, and wears the thread. No line, caption or record says whose she is;
  the player reads it from objects and faces (`design/spine-plan/09-ANNI.md`).
- **Era 2** Bet and Ilo's household: Anni (fourteen, who counts the sacks on the cart), and Tomas (eleven)
  and Aaron (nine), half-Hesh and born after the wedding (ages: author, 2026-10-22). All three are raised
  Hesh, with the thread at the wrist. Anni's register entry reads HESH (household), her brothers'
  HALF-HESH: with the father ruled off, the child follows the house. Tomas, a child, is arrested for hoarding and dies in the cell. The
  family says he went to the hills.
- **Era 3** Anni and her children, one family of ten in the old Mott house. The allocation office offers
  her the Tarn list if a Tarn man swears to her column; taking it means Ilo off her paper.
- **Era 4** Anni's grandson Joss, at the mill until it closes; his brother's line moves to the new estate
  and calls itself Tarn.
- **Era 5** Mara Haldane, Joss's granddaughter, on the tenants' committee, with the ledger; her cousin's son
  Ryan Haldane, fifteen, posts "they should all go back" under the clip.
- **Wound:** Tomas. **Lie:** "He went to the hills." The lie is the family's mercy and its trap: it kept
  the hoarding charge off their name and left Tomas with no grave.

### Krall
- The chairmen of Krall's End. **Era 2** Jonah Krall reports 340, the highest on the plain, and is made
  district chairman for it. **Era 3** his son on the allocation board. **Era 5** Councillor Dennis Krall,
  Furrow League, who chairs the clearance vote.
- **Wound:** 340. **Lie:** "The plain fed the country."

### Tesk
- A Hesh hill family, keepers of the Well. **Era 2** Dorrah Tesk hides two Haldane children in the hills
  through the worst month. **Era 3** the Well House in a yard. **Era 5** Sefa Tesk, an elder of the Well
  Assembly, who won't let a Vell girl uncover in the Well House.
- **Wound:** the Haldane children she had to send back down when the food ran out. **Lie:** "The Well
  looks after its own."

### Saro (the Vell)
- **Era 4** Kasem and Mira Saro arrive from the Vellmark; Kasem gets the job at the mill a week before it
  closes. **Era 5** Their grandson Danil Saro, seventeen, the boy in the clip, walking home on Mill Street.
- **Wound:** the brother left at the border in era 4. **Lie:** "We will go back when it's over."

## How households meet the record

Every gap the player's character opens lands on one of these households by name (pillar 3); every
household's end state is a scene the read-back can point to (pillar 5); and the next run's world keeps
the stone, the house and the table (pillar 2).

## Era 2 additions (game director, 2026-10-22; DECISIONS-2026-10-22.md)

- **Haldane.** Anni is the eldest (fourteen), Ned Furrowe's daughter by Bet, raised Hesh (spine-plan/09).
  Tomas (eleven) carried oats to Tesk's fold twice in December and lies about it to Holt (critic P2). Ilo
  makes his mark on Holt's statement to get Tomas home for his tea; it names Dorrah and the hill (critic P4).
  On the routes where Gedge comes to the yard, Ilo fetches Aaron's coat. Bet dies in the era 3 fever. Anni
  marries Oren Tesk, Dorrah's nephew; the line stays Hesh on paper until Aaron's branch calls itself Tarn.
- **Tesk.** Old Tesk has the fever in the cells; on routes where Tomas is not held, Holt sends him up the hill
  road with it. Dorrah is taken off the hill on the strength of Ilo's mark, and the six town children are
  sent down to the Furrow Church.
- **Mott.** His youngest dies of the fever on the Saturday; he wears a black band sewn on crooked.
- **Brack.** The Wether pass is signed only where Will took Tomas by the arm or walked to the wicket (critic P5).
  Edie let Bet's dresses out "before Ilo".
- **Orme.** Kit Orme, George Orme's grandson, died of the fever in November; his brown coat went to the
  Furrow Church's poor box and Bet got it for Tomas.
- **Sallis.** A Hesh hand from the Sheaf's back table, two boys, next door to the Haldanes in the yard: where
  Gedge goes when somebody speaks up for Aaron.
