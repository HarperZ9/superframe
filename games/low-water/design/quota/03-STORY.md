# The Quota, 3: story

Part of [QUOTA-DESIGN.md](../QUOTA-DESIGN.md). Beats are marked **S** (spoken: a scene with dialogue) or
**W** (told through the world: places, props, crowds, the community model's output, no dialogue). The
player plays one generation of the chosen family-trade line in each era.

## Dinners

The two hinge eras with story fights (2 and 5) have a dinner at the player's own family table, written
once per class (twelve short scenes in all). Eras 1, 3 and 4 have a shared table where every family sits:
the exchange's harvest supper, the shift table in the crowded Mott house, and the Sheaf on the night the
mill closes. Every dinner follows PITCHES.md 3a rule 3: the contempt is spoken with love.
