# Act 2, Second Water: proposal

From the director, 2026-10-16. Private. For the author's decision once Act 1 is playable. Builds on
`design/PRODUCTION.md` sections 3, 6, 7, 10 and 11.

## The question

Act 1 asked whose fault the dark is. Act 2 asks **who pays**. The cause Lamb sent up the tube at
dawn is now a fact in Gullet, and people act on it: someone is fallen, someone is lit, and the
Shelf has told its assessor to stay while the spring tides run.

## The night

19:10 to 06:20. Clear, still and freezing, -2 °C by one in the morning: the opposite of Low Night.
Every lamp works and every face can be seen, so the Lamplighters are at their strongest and the
dark, which was Lamb's friend on Night 1, has to be made. Low water at 23:46, a shorter window on
foot (gully 23:22 to 00:10). Hard frost from 01:00 holds every print. Sea smoke at 05:30.

## How Act 1 sets it

| Act 1 cause | Night 2 opens with |
|---|---|
| Wreckers | Merrow in the cells; the Low Company hostile; the Board triumphant; the Hesk wreckers arrive anyway, drawn by the story |
| The Guild | The vote banned; the Gut dark in protest; Spratt acting steward (or not, if Lamb told the hall) |
| The Trust and Board | Crake's telephone off the hook; Keel at the harbour office door all night; the Lamplighters told to watch Lamb |
| The keeper | Wenna and Sabine under the town's eye; the Brine half empty; the Congregation's relics demanded |
| The sea | Nobody fallen; everyone polite and silent |
| Divided | Each named party's state, combined |

Also carried: the lens (whole, broken, green rings gone), the fish, the lamps Lamb lit, Merrow's
copper, the Damp's hair, the Tab, the Record (every `said`, `struck`, `book` flag), favour and the
Read.

## New districts

| District | Light | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| Chancel Hill | White trial lamps | Crake in person; Mrs Pellow, who has never seen her employer's eyes; the Lamplighters' station; the Municipal substation and its second switch |
| The Gasholder | Candles and bell jars | The Congregation's church in the gasholder frame; the Old Bulb; Brother Lumen Ash's relic accounts; the procession down the hill |
| The Ropewalk | One paraffin lamp a street | Euphemia Rook's telegraph office and the 18:30 telegram's draft; the Gannet house; Hester Gannet, once the Shelf's local clerk |

The six Act 1 districts return, changed by the cause and the frost (the gutter freezes; the floor
can be reached by boat at the second low water; the stranded ships draw the Hesk men).

## Cast added

Dorran Fisk (the engineer who wrote the restrike clause, off the evening boat from Hesk), Euphemia
Rook, Mrs Pellow, Brother Lumen Ash, Hester Gannet, and the Hesk wreckers' captain. Crake in
person for the first time.

## Quests (14 new, 8 continued)

New: the telegraph book; the engineer's button; Crake's yellow ink; the Trust's ledger; the
Lamplighters' station; the procession of the Old Bulb; the boat to the Spit; the masters'
complaint (ships that saw Mother green); Mrs Pellow's errand; Brother Ash's relic accounts; the
substation's second switch; Spratt's bid for steward; the freezing of the gutter; the sale of
Merrow's copper. Continued: the low light, splinters, the tube, Gorm's book, the room, the lamps,
the Damp's hair, the fish.

## Fights (12, four set)

Set: the Hesk wreckers on the stranded ships at Second Water; the Lamplighters' station (or a
talk); zealots round the Old Bulb if a relic is taken; the frozen gutter. Optional: Lamplighters at
full strength under a clear sky, the switch room held by the Trust, the brawl over the Act 1
cause, Hesk men at the telegraph office, and others. Gorm's pole sweep can be learned if the Trust
reads Lamb as useful.

## The first day page

Between Acts 1 and 2: Lamb wakes at two in the afternoon above the Brine and sees Gullet in colour
for ten minutes, with no checks. One conversation that cannot happen in the dark (the proposal:
Wenna in daylight on the mole, looking at Mother, saying nothing about the night).

## Ending

A second dawn, and the choice of which lamps the town lights for the gale, which sets Act 3.

## Size and what it needs

- Writing: about 95,000 to 110,000 words including fights; plan, beat sheet and shared ids first
  (as Act 1), then one writer per district and one edit pass, learning from Act 1's overrun: the
  writer's brief gives each district a word ceiling and a voice-density limit up front.
- Art: three new painted districts (Chancel Hill, the Gasholder, the Ropewalk) and the frost
  variant of the six Act 1 plates; five new characters and the Hesk wreckers at the raised targets
  (PRODUCTION.md section 11).
- Engine: night 2 schedule (`@night`), boats as travel, a frozen-gutter footing, the substation's
  second power zone.

## Decisions for the author

1. Approve Act 2 as above, or change the question or the districts.
2. Whether the Hesk wreckers (strangers with no stake in the town) are the right opponents for the
   act's main set fights, or whether every fight should stay among the town's own people.
3. Whether the first day page should be playable or a short read.
