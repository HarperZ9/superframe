Adversarial story critic, 2026-10-22. Read-only for the project: this index and the six files
under `critic/` are the only files written. No content, code or other design file was edited. Another
agent was editing `content/quota/` today, so line numbers in quotes may move by a few lines.

story, that perhaps, your default output would be tailored away from, due to your friendly disposition.
Be willing to question your own outputs, and really consider the most impactful 'what the fuck!?'
moments of media history across mediums, and really polemicslly attack your own taste and output so that
you can touch on uncomfortable topics you would typically be more careful around."

Two more directions arrived mid-task, relayed by the coordinator: bosses as monstrous forms of the self
(answered in [critic/06-BOSSES.md](critic/06-BOSSES.md); the author has since adopted the inner arena,

`design/spine-plan/10-INFERNO.md` did not exist yet).

**Spine served** (author's decisions, as relayed): the boy who did not come home; the failing parent as
the moral engine; the Haldane table as the place; the famine and the falsified count as pressure;

custody; Aaron about 9; story in the writing, not in numbers.

## Summary (25 lines)

**The five strongest mechanisms**
1. Withheld sight with the sound kept (The Zone of Interest, Son of Saul): the cells beside the player, never opened.
2. Complicity through the player's own verb (Spec Ops, Shadow of the Colossus): the pen, the bar, the arm.
3. The parent's choice approached sideways (Beloved, Sophie's Choice, Maus's "Richieu"): Ilo's mark, Bet's misnaming.
4. The perpetrator's account as the frame, broken late (Mouthwashing, Silent Hill 2): the self-lies, proved.
5. The witness pressed while working (Shoah's barbershop, The Look of Silence's eye tests): Anni at forty.

**The top five softness findings**
1. The four days are cut: the jailer's line jumps from day 41 to "A week later." (`q_coda.lwd:17`) past the death on day 45 (`q_readback.lwd:27`).
2. Courage costs nothing on screen: "Holt laughs through his nose and waves the barrier up, as if it bored him." (`q_ned.lwd:187`).
3. The saintly victim: "Sorry to have bothered you." (`q_ned.lwd:120`); "Thank you for the seed." (`q_will.lwd:146`); the charge is always false.
4. The plan censors the victim: the witness lint bans "guilty, murderer, forgive" (`spine-plan/02-WITNESS.md:258`); she may be unkind only "in a small way" (`:192-193`).
5. Redemption by completionism: a later run earns "T. HALDANE" on a stone (`q_props.lwd:58`); "Someone in era 5 can do one true thing for Tomas" (`spine-plan/01-SPINE.md:60-61`).

**The top five proposed moments**
1. P3, the four days: the player keeps the cells, fills the water jug, hears the boy say the prayer wrong; "intestinal illness" is true.
2. P4, the paper in the stove: Ilo, who cannot read, makes his mark on a statement naming Tesk's fold to get his son home; the son dies anyway.
3. P1, the promise at two: the inspector's kindness walks Tomas to Holt; the family remembers the handshake.
4. P9, Aaron in the uniform: the brother told the lie joins the militia; "I haven't got a brother, Sergeant. He went to the hills."
5. P14, the Tesk page: the archive proves Tomas did carry oats; the player who fought denial wants to tear it out.

**Bosses and the inferno** (files 06, 07): winning a self-boss is doing the act the lie excused; no devil acts in the world; the Adversary is the Accuser, a clerk who keeps the perfect record.

## Method and limits

- **Facts.** Most entries are from memory at high confidence. Facts checked by web search on
  2026-10-22 are marked **verified** in files 01 and 02; one is marked **UNVERIFIED** (Pathologic's
  theatre framing). Two corrections came from checking: in Mouthwashing it is Jimmy who crashed the ship,
  which a first recollection had blurred; the End of Evangelion letters were, by one fan source, written
  by staff, which is reported and not settled.
- **Read-aloud test.** All forty proposal lines were read as spoken lines and run through
  `articulate dialogue_check` with theme words (guilt, justice, memory, truth, persecution, innocence).
  Result: gate passed, 0 of 40 lines flagged, 0 epigram lines. No voice sheets were supplied, so voice
  distinctiveness was not scored. **Check the check:** the tool missed an antithesis I can see,
  KASEM: "I did not ask for a turn. I asked for the shift." By ear, four more lines lean toward a closing
  zinger and should be read by the author before approval: "It says you're his father."; "Everything's
  for sale after the week."; "That's the one nice thing I know about your grandad."; and Sefa's "to make
  him easier to like", which comes close to stating the theme. A pass from the tool proves only that the
  pattern rules did not fire.
- **The Accuser's five lines** (file 07) were run the same way: gate passed, 0 flagged. By ear, "I don't
  add anything. I never have. I only keep it." comes close to stating the theme and should be read
  aloud by the author.
- **Scenes described plainly.** No scene is re-enacted. Sexual violence in Mouthwashing and Berserk is
  named, not described. Children's deaths are named as facts.
- **Does not prove.** The audit shows where the writing is soft by the twelve tests; it does not show a
  player would feel it that way. The proposals are untested in play.
