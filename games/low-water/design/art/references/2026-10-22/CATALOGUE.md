One row per file, 01 to 77, as the files sit in this folder. Each image was opened and looked at on
2026-10-22 by the art director's archivist. Descriptions say only what is visible. An artist or work is
named only where a signature, watermark or caption is visible, or where the work is well known; the
confidence of each attribution is given in brackets. Everything else is "unattributed".

These are other people's works kept for private study. The image files are gitignored and never
shipped. Only method is borrowed.
