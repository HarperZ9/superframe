# Inferno 7: the senses, per boss, and the care that holds

Part of [../10-INFERNO.md](../10-INFERNO.md). **PROPOSAL throughout.** Built on the looks
(`design/sensory/01-LOOKS.md`), the morphs (`02-MORPHS.md`), the surreal grammar (`03-SURREAL.md`), the
senses (`04-SENSES.md`) and care (`05-CARE.md`). Those files govern where this one is silent.

## 1. The inner arena's base

- **Look.** The dream look, L6 (`01-LOOKS.md`): the hour's palette drifted, saturation down to 55%,
  looser stroke, the light model's terms kept. Added for the inner arena: the darks take aquatint grain,
  the granular tone of Goya's etched plates ([01-SOURCES.md](01-SOURCES.md) section 10), as a paper
  texture in the shadow band only.
- **Entry.** The stove door opens. Sound first, as the grammar requires (`03-SURREAL.md`, rule 4): the
  kitchen's reverb gives way to the long "space" room while the stove's tick stays dry; 0.5 s later the
  picture breaks from the stove's slot outward. Exit is the reverse, into morning light.
- **The anchor rule holds in every fight.** Each boss names one object that stays in the base look,
  unchanged, inside the break. It is how the player knows where they are.
- **Inside the arena the grammar's size and time bounds are lifted** (rule 10 allows it in a dream
  scene), and the one-break-per-view rule is kept: a boss's morph is the break; nothing else breaks in
  the same view.
- **Leitmotifs.** The existing motifs carry the bosses; no new theme is written for a monster. A boss is
  heard as one of the town's own motifs going wrong.
- **Haptics.** The existing vocabulary and its rules (`04-SENSES.md`, section 6): nothing carried only by
  the motors, and never a fake heartbeat.

## 3. Care limits that stay

- **Flash.** The flash and red rule binds every phase and morph (`05-CARE.md`, section 2): at most three
  flashes a second anywhere, red transitions counted as flashes, one a second under "Reduce bright
  flashes". No morph is a luminance flash; it is a change of look (`03-SURREAL.md`, rule 9). The Doom's
  stove mouth, the Long Barn's chute and the van light in Order are the three risks; each has a steady
  form under the setting.
- **The surreal never draws a people** (`03-SURREAL.md`, rule 8), and no boss is drawn as a starved body,
  a child's body or a wound (`design/critic/07-INFERNO.md`, section 3). Gore is not used: nothing in the
  inner arena bleeds.
- **Children.** Through consequence only (`design/QUOTA-DESIGN.md:34`): a shadow, a breath, a sleeve, a
  moved chair. No child is drawn in any inner arena.
- **Content warning additions** to `design/quota/07-PRODUCTION.md:57-62`: nightmare sequences with
  demonic and religious imagery; a parent's violence experienced as the child (Friday); the persecution
  of a people in religious language.
- **Scene notes** (`05-CARE.md`, section 7) before Friday, Nearest One and the Adversary; stepping past is
  a story option the record notes neutrally, and a stepped-past boss counts as neither force nor
  resolution: it is kept as unfaced, and returns in the Descent in its first phase.
- **Steady world.** Every boss's morph shows inside an era-window frame with its sound signature and a
  caption (`05-CARE.md`, section 7). The fight is unchanged.
- **Haptics** under the 0 to 100% slider; none carries information sound or picture does not carry.
- **Loudness.** The inner arena is mixed to the dialogue profile (-16 LUFS, `05-CARE.md`); the Descent may
  not exceed the granary door's peak.
- **Captions** for every boss line, with the speaker named as the skill (SUM, BALLAST) or as the boss's
  town name, never as a demon.
