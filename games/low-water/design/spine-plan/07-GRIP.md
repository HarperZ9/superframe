# Spine plan 7: what makes it grip, and the plan attacked by its own author

Part of [../SPINE-PLAN.md](../SPINE-PLAN.md). The author's direction, relayed verbatim by the
coordinator, 2026-10-22: "Ensure to cover the elements of a good gripping story, that perhaps, your
default output would be tailored away from, due to your friendly disposition. Be willing to question
your own outputs, and really consider the most impactful 'what the fuck!?' moments of media history
across mediums, and really polemicslly attack your own taste and output so that you can touch on
uncomfortable topics you would typically be more careful around."

A separate critic is studying the great shocks across media and will attack this plan after it lands.
This file does the first attack from inside. All PROPOSAL.

## 3. Sample: Tomas with an edge (PROPOSAL)

At the post, before Holt's car comes, on the routes where Holt arrives:

> GEDGE: What's in that one?
> TOMAS: Barley. Seed. Same as the last time you asked.
> GEDGE: Don't you get clever.
> TOMAS: I'm not. If I was clever I'd be in Osby.

Holt's car comes up behind the hut while Gedge is still red. Holt has heard the last line.

## 4. What this file does not prove

- Nothing here was played. Whether a beat grips is a reader's and a player's finding, never the
  writer's. The build plan sets a read-aloud and a cold-read test for each of the revised beats
  (06-BUILD.md, phase 3).
- The hostile reread is the same writer reading his own work. The independent critic's attack is the
  check that this one cannot be.
