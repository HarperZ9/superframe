## 4. The witness: Aaron, year 46

**Where.** Aaron's kitchen on the far bank, the Tarn estate, year 46. Aaron is 43, still in uniform.
**Listener:** his son Rob (11), who found a blue thread in a drawer. **Opening object:** the relief bowl
stamped with the district's number, which Aaron took when the Mott house was cleared
(`content/quota/q_props.lwd:29`).

**He tells it clean, and the clean version breaks.** He starts with the common memory: the bad winter,
his brother who went to the hills, the new flats. A small thing breaks in (Langer's deep memory,
`design/spine-plan/02-WITNESS.md:67-73`): the boots by the door, the handcart's wheel, the smell of the
church hall. He goes back to the clean version each time, a little less well.

**The guest, by class.** Inspectors: Pell (46), surveying the estate for the bank. Militia: Will (70),
retired, with Aaron's long-service paper. Clerks: Walter (74), with the tenancy transfer. Correspondents:
Lena Ashby (64), collecting memories for a book on the rebuilding. Doctors: Clara Lind (60), for Rob's
cough. Millers: Harriet Orme (66), the landlord's agent, for the rent.

**Reach.** He saw: the riot from the line; the fire from the line; his mother's death; the Sholls carried
down the stairs by his squad, where Will chose it. He heard: "he went to the hills"; Anni and Ilo through
the wall. He never knew: Pell's flat; the margin note; what Ned swore. **Omission:** he skips the Sholls,
and the checker records the skip. **Echo:** Order's line, if the player heard it, plays as Aaron says
"We kept them back off the fire."

**Last beat, an effect.** Rob asks if he can keep the thread. Aaron takes it, says it's dirty, and puts it
in the stamped bowl on the high shelf. The guest sees where it goes.

**Record.** `@witness e3.w.aaron`, `who: AARON`, `listener: ROB`, place `q3w_far_bank`, `opens: prop
q.relief_bowl`.
