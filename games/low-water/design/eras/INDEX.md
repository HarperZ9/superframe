# The eras: index (package W1-F)

Game director, 2026-10-22. Eras 1, 3, 4 and 5 designed to the spine at build detail, so the write,
plates, cast, systems and sound packages of waves 2 to 7 can start (`design/COMPLETION.md:386`). Era 2 is
built (`content/quota/`) and is mapped here only so the beat map is whole. New material is **PROPOSAL**,
taken under the author's blanket approval and reversible. Every sample line waits for the author.

**The author's standing directions this package serves, all 2026-10-22, verbatim where quoted:**
- The spine: "the boy who did not come home, the failing parent as the moral engine, the Haldane table as
  the place" (`design/SPINE-PLAN.md:11-13`).
- Persecution, ethnic cleansing and genocide as "highlighted topics in the plotline and charcaters"
  (`design/SPINE-PLAN.md:16-17`).
- Plague and illness, a hellish world with "the trauma and pain up to 11"
  (`design/spine-plan/DECISIONS-2026-10-22.md:94`), carried by rhythm.
- Fates decided by named people in written scenes; the inner arena of self-lies, the great sins, the
  Adversary, the Golem and other mythic creatures (`design/spine-plan/10-INFERNO.md:16-29`).
- "Story is in the writing" (`design/SPINE-PLAN.md:14-15`): no figure on the player's surface.
- Abuse shown on screen, with two lines held (`design/spine-plan/DECISIONS-2026-10-22.md:124-131`).
- Attack soft defaults; dialogue that sounds human (`design/spine-plan/07-GRIP.md:6-11`;
  `design/DIALOGUE-STANDARD.md`).
- Wave 0 from the author, 2026-10-23: all six classes (D5), hand-built art with first-party tools (D1),
  generated and disclosed voices (D4) (`design/spine-plan/DECISIONS-2026-10-22.md:143-149`).
