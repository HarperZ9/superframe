## 4. The ending, and the witness that is a table

**The commemoration (e5.s14).** Mara is given the floor. She says what came down to her about a boy. What
she has depends on the chains (index section 5): with chain H she has where he lies and "I carried him",
and says it; the channel cuts it to "the Haldane woman says the police carried a Hesh hoarder", which it calls
proof that the Hesh lad was the hoarder after all. Without it she tells "he went to the hills", believing it. On the Tomas-alive routes she reads
a letter from over the ridge, in Heshen she cannot read, and says so.

**One true thing, late, at a cost, refused (`design/spine-plan/01-SPINE.md:59-63`).** Every route has one:

| Who holds the words | The true thing | The cost | How it is refused |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ann (chain B) | Says Will's words to Leon at the door | Her son and her grandfather's name | It moves him until Monday; the clearance goes ahead on Monday |
| Tom (chain F) | Says them to Leon at the door, with his council lanyard on | His post | "Your lot signed for everything. Back behind the tape, Mr Furrowe." |
| Mara (chain H) | Says them at the commemoration | The committee's grant | Quoted on the channel as proof the Hesh lad was a hoarder |
| Nobody | The player keeps Dorrah's page | Tomas's clean name in public | The League posts it |

**The table (e5.s16; `design/CINEMATICS.md:205-213`).** The dealer has dressed the window for commemoration
week: the door table, five old bowls from a house clearance, chairs pulled up, gruel cast in resin, steam
from a humidifier under the table (critic P12, `design/critic/05-PROPOSALS-LATER.md:112-132`). It is the
era 2 dream's table (`content/quota/q_dreams.lwd:19`). The player's era 5 man stops at the window, or
walks on; the camera holds while the player presses nothing.

**Witness record.** `@witness e5.w.table` has no speaker: its beats are close looks, each answering the run
(the hinge marks from era 1; the goat with its head turned back; A.H. with a line through it; the thread
round the leg where the world record holds one; Ilo's spoons ticketed beside it). The dealer's three lines
are its only words. witnesscheck's rules hold: no verdict, no count, unskippable on a first ending, an effect
for the last beat: a customer asks if the table is for sale, and the dealer says everything is, after the week.

## 9. The inner arena, the Descent and the Adversary

**The traits pass (2026-10-23, design/TRAITS.md).** Every boss is now one of the character's traits embodied, and the
self-lie is spoken by that trait. Raising the trait makes its being bigger; each ending changes the trait in him
(force cuts it back, terms keep it, losing gives it him). The names below lead with the trait.

| Line | Boss | Armed by | Without force | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| All six | SPITE, as Everybody Says, from the share on the player's phone | The night after the clip | Endure at the stone on the verge | `design/spine-plan/inferno/03-BESTIARY-B.md:267-300` |
| Militia | LOYALTY, as Order, returns for Leon, three generations in the wall | The night after the stop | Drop the baton, endure | `design/spine-plan/inferno/03-BESTIARY-B.md:233-265` |

**The Descent**, the night before the clearance day (design/TRAITS.md section 5): four gates, one per great
trait (HARDNESS, FEAR, SUSPICION, AUTHORITY), from the weakest in him to the strongest. A gate is armed when a
trait under it was fed to 7 or more, cut back by force, or lost to; its being is the great trait's body
fighting as the most-fed trait, with the next one's blows. A gate where nothing was fed is a yard in snow with
the stove door open. JEALOUSY, DREAD, SHAME and CONTEMPT are met only here. **The Adversary** is the Host in
three movements: the Accuser, who stood at his shoulder for every fight and speaks his fed traits back to him in
their own voices; the Host, the combined monstrosity whose limbs are the traits he cut back and the traits that
took him; the whisper that stops when it is recognised, and "It will be back." No outcome changes the clearance
day's people.
