## 4. The witness: Ilo at the Sheaf, year 12

**When and where.** Day 40 of era 2, evening, the Sheaf's back room. Rent night: Abel collects the Haldane
barns rent at the back table on every route, because a Hesh tenant does not knock at the Furrowe front door.
The next day Ilo tells the short version (`content/quota/q_sheaf.lwd:15-21`), so the player hears it twice,
once whole and once as a joke. **Listener:** Tomas (11), sent to fetch his dad. **Opening object:** the
census slip in Ilo's coat, HALDANE, I., AFTER THE FARM, soft as cloth.

**The guest, by class.** The era 1 man of the player's line, old, at the Sheaf for an ordinary reason:

| Class | Guest (age) | Why he is there | Recognition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Inspectors | Abel (75) | Collecting the rent at the back table | Ilo names "Mr Furrowe in the door with his hat in his hands"; Abel corrects the spelling |
| Militia | Josh Brack (64) | His usual stool at the bar | Ilo tells the frost list; on the sent-up route he names Ketti Sallis and does not look round |
| Clerks | Edwin Mott (70), retired | A half of mild on his own, every Wednesday | Ilo shows the slip; Mott knows his own hand |
| Correspondents | Clement Ashby (57) | Waiting for the Arlen paper off the late train | Ilo quotes the Gazette's line about the toast |
| Doctors | Dr Oskar Lind (62) | Called to a man with a cough at the back | Ilo tells the fit-to-travel papers |
| Millers | George Orme (64) | Sending his foreman in with the carters' pay; George in the doorway | Ilo tells the back store and Kel Tesk |

**Reach.** Ilo saw: the loft, the census table, the supper through the window, the hands going up the hill
road, Osby fair. He heard: the board's rule, from Abel; Ketti Sallis's death, from Dorrah at the cup, on the route where she went up. He
never knew: what was said in the board room; what Abel said to Mott after; what Bet told Edie Brack.
**Omission beat (declared unwitnessed by choice):** Ilo never tells Tomas that he knew whose Anni was. The
checker records it as a beat he skips, as critic P8 proposes for Anni (`design/critic/05-PROPOSALS-LATER.md:19-21`).

**Echo.** Fond of Them's line, if the player heard it at the board, plays once, quiet, as Ilo reaches the
doorway; Ilo talks over it. **Last beat, an effect.** Tomas asks why five bowls when there were three of
them. Ilo says his mother said buy five. Abel counts the rent, coin by coin, and pushes one coin back
toward Ilo without a word. Ilo leaves it there, and the scene ends on the coin and Tomas looking at it.
**Record:** `@witness e1.w.ilo`, `listener: TOMAS`, `place: q_sheaf`, `opens: e1.w.ilo.open` (the end barn key on its string); beats
keyed to `f.e1_sallis`, `f.e1_kel`, `said.e1_*` and the era 1 self-lies.
