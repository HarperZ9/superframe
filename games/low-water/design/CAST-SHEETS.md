Rules carried from CHARACTERS-ART: heads 1.2 to 1.5 times natural; one exaggerated feature each;
one large pale mass against a dark one, readable at quay distance under sodium; two people who
stand together in a scene differ in value where they touch (the pairs to check are listed at the
end). Colours below are the paraffin colours; under sodium only their values survive, so each record
gives the value (0 dark to 1 pale) of its two main masses.

Priority: **A** Act 1 principals and set-fight opponents; **B** Act 1 supporting cast; **C** Act 2.

**Wenna Tallow.** Exaggerated: the oversized head on a small round back. Costume: the oxblood
keeper's coat, too long, cuffs turned back twice; under it a grey knitted gansey; a dry oilskin on
the Brine peg (a separate prop, it matters as evidence); boots. Hair: crop, white, cut with a knife
(uneven). Face: small nose, deep lines, lids heavy, mouth thin and set; paraffin-burn scars on both
backs of the hands (texture). Pale mass: the white crop and the face; the coat is the dark mass
(value 0.25; oxblood reads only in paraffin). Prop: none in hand; a brass key on a string at her
neck. Style: small steps, one hand on any rail, looks up at Mother whenever the mole is in frame.

**Absalom Keel.** Exaggerated: height and narrowness, and the water. Costume: navy greatcoat black
with wet, to the ankle, buttoned to the throat; the harbourmaster's peaked cap that funnels water
down the back of the neck; boots in a puddle. Hair: none visible under the cap; grey sideburns.
Face: long, grey "of a thumb kept in a bath", long jaw, small mouth, eyes pale and wet. Pale mass:
the face and the white cap badge; everything else value 0.15. Effect: a constant drip from cuffs
and cap brim and a puddle decal where he stands (the character lead's open part); the drip never
stops in any clip. Prop: a brass speaking trumpet tucked under the arm on the quay; the lump hammer
in the 04:50 fight. Style: rigidly upright; turns the whole body, not the head.

**Ottilie Crake** (Act 2, in person). Exaggerated: precision; she is the one person drawn without
grotesque excess, and that is her strangeness. Costume: grey that reads the same under every light
(value 0.5 in paraffin, sodium and white), a fitted coat to mid-calf, gloves, yellow-lensed
spectacles (the one hot point on her). Hair: band (a smooth grey roll). Face: straight nose, firm
chin, lids level. Pale mass: none; she is a single mid value, which is why she disappears under
sodium and stands out under white. Prop: a fountain pen with yellow ink. Style: still; hands folded
at the waist when listening.

**Sabine Brine.** Exaggerated: width and quickness. Costume: sleeves rolled to the elbow, a striped
apron over a dark skirt, a shawl crossed and tied behind; the pickling fork through the bun (the
character lead's open part). Hair: dark bun. Face: wide mouth with a ready smile, small bright eyes,
broad cheeks. Pale mass: the apron (value 0.75) against the skirt (0.2). Prop: the chalk and the
slate cloth; the bread peel at the oven. Style: never still; weight on the balls of the feet.

**Tub Merrow.** Exaggerated: short and wide, wet to the thigh. Costume: yellow oilskin smock (value
0.6 under sodium: his pale mass), dark trousers dark-wet to the thigh (a wetness mask to the thigh,
drying over the night), a rope coil on one shoulder, a cold pipe. Hair: cap (a knitted cap). Face:
flat nose, weathered, short beard. Prop: the sack; a boathook in Act 2. Style: stands with feet apart
as if on a deck; never looks at the person he is talking to.

**Pip Gannet.** Exaggerated: long legs. Costume: a short skirt over boys' boots, an apron, a
bodice, a shawl; water line on her stockings at the knee (her tide-reading). Hair: crop, wild, tied
back with string. Face: freckled, wide eyes, quick mouth. Pale mass: the apron and the face. Prop:
the brass canister. Style: fidgets; rubs the backs of her legs before the tide turns.

**The Lamplighters** share one costume: a long dark Municipal coat (value 0.15), a cap with a brass
badge, a brass pole longer than the wearer (two-handed, its brass the pale mass in the dark), a
notebook in a breast pocket. They differ in proportions, palette accents and one prop each:
- **Gorm:** the sergeant's two stripes in pale braid on the cuff; the open book always in her left
  hand; pencil behind the ear. Face: square, still, eyebrows raised (she speaks only in questions).
- **Hobb:** youngest; two lit lanterns, one in each hand (pole slung on the back); coat too big at
  the shoulder. Face: long and anxious. Style: lanterns held out from the body, never lowered.
- **Fewell:** a new pole in Act 2 if struck (a bandage on the hand). Face: heavy moustache.
- **Strand:** counts under his breath; a pale scarf (his value split from Fewell when they fight as
  a pair).
- **Bright:** spectacles; Act 2 mole guard.
- **Coe:** thickset; a muffler; Act 2 switch-room guard.

**Agnes Spratt.** Exaggerated: a forward-thrust chin. Costume: eel-wife's apron, oilcloth sleeves
to the elbow, a headscarf, a string of eels over one shoulder. Pale mass: the oilcloth sleeves
(0.7). Prop: a gutting knife; the scales on her stall.

**Mab and Noll Whelk.** One costume, two palettes (the character lead's plan): gutters' aprons,
rubber gloves to the elbow, knives. Mab's apron pale (0.75), Noll's darker (0.4), so side by side at
their trestle they split. Exaggerated: big heads leaning toward each other; they speak toward each
other's ear.

**Ned Shadwell.** Exaggerated: soot. Costume: a leather apron black with soot, shirt the colour of
smoke, a soot-black face with pale eye-rings (his pale mass, small and startling). Prop: a smoking
pipe of black shag; a rake for the smoke pit. Style: looks up at the beams more than at people.

**Sister Gauze.** Exaggerated: the bell jar worn over the head (the character lead's open part),
fogged at the mouth. Costume: a grey habit, a white wimple seen through the glass (pale mass). Style:
moves slowly; the jar catches every light in frame.

**Tobit Quill.** A choirboy's white surplice over a jersey, too long; a candle. Pale mass: the
surplice. Style: sings with his whole body.

**Ferris Lugg.** Exaggerated: height and the stoop of a tall man under rafters; very long arms.
Costume: salvor's oilskins, dark (0.2), a pale canvas sack slung (pale mass), a crowbar, rope.
Face: long, mild, thin lips (he whispers). Style: bends to speak; never raises his arms above the
shoulder except to fight.

**Nan Cockle.** Exaggerated: age; stoop past 20 degrees, a stick (the character lead's open part).
Costume: men's sea boots, three skirts kilted up, a man's coat, a bucket. Pale mass: a white cap.
Face: a nut of a face, toothless smile. Style: walks only on the shingle bar's line; points with the
stick.

**Ysolde Penhallow.** Exaggerated: the cap and the set of the shoulders of someone who has stood
at a helm for thirty years. Costume: master's reefer jacket, a peaked cap, a muffler; a log book.
Pale mass: the muffler (0.75). Style: stands square to the bay, gives bearings with an arm.

**Orrin Teague.** Exaggerated: luggage. Costume: a travelling coat and hat from nine years ago,
buckled valise always at his feet, a hat he takes off to the ferry. Pale mass: his collar.

**Dilly Rudd.** A floor child: oversized boots, a lantern, a slate on a string round her neck (her
book on the fish). Pale mass: the slate's chalk marks and the lantern.

**Gill.** Spratt's runner: a long apron to the ankle with straps (Lamb lifts him by them), a cap,
a smokehouse ticket in the pocket.

**Captain Hale Dunlin** (the small thing in the gutter). Marrow's proportions, older; a captain's
coat with buttons gone green, a blindfold slack round the neck, a pipe. Rendered only in the
gutter's violet band, and never in any other light.

**Dorran Fisk.** Exaggerated: neatness, ruined by the coast road (mud to the knee on a good suit).
Costume: a town suit, a bowler, a satchel of specifications, a pencil behind the ear. Pale mass: his
collar and the papers. Style: holds the satchel to his chest.

**Mrs Pellow.** Black housekeeper's dress, white apron and cap (pale mass), a covered basket on her
errand, her employer's gloves at the dawn meeting.

**Euphemia Rook.** Exaggerated: a long neck bent toward the sounder. Costume: a telegraphist's
sleeve protectors (pale), a dark dress, ink on the right forefinger, a pencil in the hair.

**Brother Lumen Ash.** A treasurer's black cassock, a white collar, a ledger always under the arm,
a bell jar he does not wear. Pale mass: the ledger's edges and the collar.

**Hester Gannet.** Not seen in Act 2. For Act 3: a shawl, fingerless mittens, a typewriter ribbon
tin in her pocket.

**Sallis** and the **town strippers** (Pruett, Boddy, Hask, the Penroses): wardens' dark oilskins,
boathooks and a chain; Sallis has a Board armband (pale). **Captain Pardew** and the **Hesk crew**
(Twine, Fenna, Gage, Shilling): pea jackets, knitted caps, bars and hooks; the captain wears a clean
collar and reading spectacles (he has read the claim). One body each for the two crews, with
palette variations; only the leaders need their own records.

**Hal Dace and Orme.** Masters of the barges: reefers like Penhallow's but older, one with a pale
muffler, one without, so the two read apart.
