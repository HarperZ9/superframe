A metal's colour is the light it hands back, band by band, so a different lamp or a missing band changes what you see.

In plain words

10x

White light is many colours at once. A metal hands some bands back strongly and swallows others; the bands it hands back are the colour you see.

Your eye squeezes all those bands into three signals. So two metals with different bands can look alike under one lamp and come apart under another.
