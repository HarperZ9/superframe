# CIE data

- `CIE_xyz_1931_2deg.csv`: CIE 1931 colour-matching functions, 2 degree observer,
  360-830 nm at 1 nm. International Commission on Illumination (CIE), 2019.
  DOI 10.25039/CIE.DS.xvudnb9b. Source: CIE 018:2019, Table 6.
- `CIE_std_illum_D65.csv`: CIE standard illuminant D65, 1 nm steps.
  Source: ISO/CIE 11664-2:2022, Table B.1.

Both files are unmodified copies from files.cie.co.at. Their metadata records give the
