Take it apart

Mix pictures by weight, comb them like marbled ink, then measure how far the result sits from each one.

In plain words

10x

Each picture you add is a voice in the mix. Its weight says how loud it is, and its eight channels say which parts of it you keep: shape, colour, texture and the rest.

Marbling drags a comb of tines through the mix, the way a marbler drags a comb through ink floating on water. More tines, finer stripes.

The report measures how far the result sits from each source. Those numbers help a person decide; they never decide on their own.
