# Vertical slice outline: Lamp Quay, 23:00 to 00:00

Private draft, 2026-10-16. The script is `lamp_quay_2300.lwd` (LWD/0). A generated reading copy is
`SLICE-READING-COPY.md`. The playable prototype is `game/`.

## Shape

One district, one hour of game time, played in about 45 to 60 minutes (an estimate; not measured on
players). The hour is a hub on the quay with eight places. Every action costs minutes; the night
does not wait. At midnight the Shelf's tube demands a provisional cause, and the slice ends on the
verdict the player can support.

## Provisional verdicts at midnight

| Verdict | Needs | Reached by |

## Play notes

A random walker over the script (the writers' lint, `tools/lwd_check.mjs`) mostly ends on "wreckers" or
"none yet": wandering without a plan gets you the claim as filed, which is the point. The Trust
verdict needs the most planning: the office (by Keel's key, fog or a blackout) and the lamp room,
inside the hour. Whether players find the other verdicts often enough is for play sessions.
